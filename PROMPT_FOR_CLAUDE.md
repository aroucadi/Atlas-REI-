# Strategic Consultation: Sourcing Product-Market Fit & Funding for Atlas REI

This document contains the prompt to feed into Claude (along with `PRODUCT.md`, `README.md`, and `INVESTORS.md` from the root of this project) to get a highly precise, strategic recommendation for our next business moves.

---

## The Claude Prompt

Copy the following text block and paste it into Claude:

```text
You are a top-tier venture capitalist, Y Combinator startup mentor, and growth strategist specializing in PropTech, B2B SaaS, and Enterprise GenAI.

I am providing you with the documentation files for Atlas REI:
1. PRODUCT.md (Detailed value proposition, technical solutions, and business pain points)
2. README.md (Monorepo architecture, features, and tech stack)
3. INVESTORS.md (Strategic accelerators and regional ecosystems)

Please analyze these documents carefully and advise us on our strategic GTM and fundraising direction. Specifically, answer the following:

1. TARGET MARKET MATCH
Analyze our four proposed segments:
- US Mid-market Multifamily Syndicators
- UK/Europe Commercial Retail/Office Funds
- GCC (UAE/Qatar/Saudi) Sovereign Wealth/Family Offices & Developers
- APAC (Singapore) REITs & Asset Managers
Which market has the highest immediate willingness to pay and the shortest sales cycle for an unmocked, citation-grounded underwriting engine? Explain why.

2. ACCELERATOR / INCUBATOR SELECTION
Rank the accelerators we are targeting by feasibility and impact:
- Y Combinator (YC W27/S27)
- Startup Qatar / QDB (Middle East)
- Misk Accelerator (Saudi Arabia)
- Startup SG / EntrePass (Singapore)
- Innovate UK (UK)
- Fit 4 Start (Luxembourg)
Which one should we submit our primary application to first, and how should we customize our pitch to stand out for that specific cohort?

3. PRODUCT ADAPTATION
To maximize our chance of getting accepted into your recommended accelerator and closing our first 5 paid design partners, what product adaptations must we build? (e.g., how should we expand packages/country-pack, what integrations like Excel export or comp APIs should we prioritize?)

4. THE GTM OUTREACH SCRIPT
Provide a refined cold-outreach messaging sequence (LinkedIn and email) tailored to the persona in your recommended target market that will guarantee we book user discovery calls.

Provide a highly concrete, actionable, and zero-bullshit roadmap. Focus on high-velocity execution and capital efficiency.
```
