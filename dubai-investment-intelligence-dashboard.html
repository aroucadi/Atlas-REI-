Section 06 · Vision 2040

Some districts have policy wind. Others are simply cheap.

Your budget leaves very little room for buying “good enough” stock in places with no rerating narrative. This section isolates where the city’s long-term infrastructure story actually intersects with your opportunity set.

Strongest policy alignment

Dubai South has the clearest strategic tailwind because the DWC airport expansion is real, funded, and directly tied to the long-term urban-growth map.

Highest practical alignment

JVC is not the cleanest Vision 2040 story, but it still wins the practical race because liquidity, rental depth, and yield visibility are already present.

Where affordability can mislead

Discovery Gardens and International City can look compelling on price alone. Without a strong corridor catalyst, they behave more like affordability trades than conviction-led rerating plays.
